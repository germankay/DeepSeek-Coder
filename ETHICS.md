# AI Ethics & Safety Statement - CyberCode Studio

## Ethics Principles
1. **Sovereignty & Security**: Empower developers with localized, secure code assistance without compromising proprietary data.
2. **Defensive AI Security**: CyberCode Studio AI models are strictly aligned towards vulnerability detection, remediation, and defensive software engineering.
3. **Bias Mitigation & Red-Teaming**: Models undergo rigorous red-teaming tests against jailbreaks and instruction manipulation.
